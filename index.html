9Inventory Ledger

Five transactions, five entries — strictly 1:1

INVENTORY_TRANSACTION is what event happened (a warehouse receipt). INVENTORY_ENTRY is what inventory actually changed. Every entry's transaction_id is FK + UNIQUE — one entry per transaction, no exceptions.

🔒 Ledger rule: once POSTED, a transaction/entry pair is historical evidence. It is never edited directly — a correction uses a reversal transaction plus a new correct one. Ledger = history. Current Position = current answer.
