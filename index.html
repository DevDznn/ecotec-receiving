0Detailed Business Scenario

Two Excel files, two packages, one ASN

Shipment 3137323 arrives as two independently traceable packages. Product Package 1 has 10 different iPhone models. Parts Package 2 has screws plus batteries assigned to different iPhone models. The ASN immediately creates pending inventory transactions for the expected items. Those pending transactions are traceable in the ledger, but they do not affect inventory balance until resolved to a posted receipt.

01
MASTER DATA

Each SKU already has its own master item

The product file maps each phone SKU to its own ITEM_MASTER and PRODUCT record. Each product keeps its own model number, storage, color, year, region, and category. The parts file maps screw and battery SKUs to ITEM_MASTER + PART.

POST ACCEPTED GOODS

Pending ledger transactions are resolved by the receiving result

In the normal scenario, all pending product and part transactions become POSTED. In the missing-phone scenario, 9 phone transactions post while the missing unit stays pending until the shortage is dispositioned. In the IMEI mismatch scenario, only the mismatched phone stays pending while the other 9 post; customer approval of an IMEI correction resolves that one pending transaction to POSTED, while ship-back resolves it without inventory posting.

Serialized phone definition

Brand, series, model number, storage, color, model year, region, and product category describe the product that the 10 individual phone assets belong to.

10Inventory Ledger

ASN transactions enter the ledger as PENDING

🔒 Ledger rule: the ASN creates the expected inventory transaction immediately with PENDING status. Pending transactions are traceable but do not affect stock. Only transactions resolved to POSTED create INVENTORY_ENTRY and increase on-hand inventory. Exception units can remain pending or be resolved without posting.
